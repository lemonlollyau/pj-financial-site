How it works

A clear process, with a broker you can reach at every step.

  1. Talk it through

    Call, email or meet us. We’ll ask about your plans, income and commitments, and give you our Credit Guide.

  2. Compare your options

    We search our lender panel and explain the trade-offs between rates, fees, features and turnaround times.

  3. Apply and get approved

    We prepare the application, gather the paperwork with you and deal with the lender until it’s approved.

  4. Settle and stay in touch

    We work with your settlement agent to get you to settlement, then check in as your needs change.
