Privacy and complaints

Privacy and complaints.

How we handle your personal information, and what to do if you’re unhappy with our service.

Your privacy

We collect personal information so we can provide credit assistance, such as finding a suitable loan and applying for it on your behalf. We handle your information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and share it only with the parties needed to arrange your finance, such as lenders, our credit licensee BLSSA Pty Ltd and the service providers who support our work.

For a copy of our full Privacy Policy, or to access or correct the information we hold about you, call us on (08) 9437 3666 or email admin@pjfinancialsolutions.com.au.

Our Credit Guide

Before we provide credit assistance we’ll give you our Credit Guide. It sets out who we are, who our licensee is, how we’re paid and how to make a complaint. Ask us for a copy at any time.

Making a complaint

If you’re unhappy with any part of our service, please tell us. You can raise a complaint by phone, email, letter or in person, and there’s no charge to do so.

Complaints Officer: Paul Channell, Finance DirectorP & J Financial Solutions, PO Box 594, South Fremantle WA 6162Phone (08) 9437 3666Email paul@pjfs.com.au

You can also contact our credit licensee directly:

BLSSA Pty Ltd Complaints OfficersLevel 15, 360 Elizabeth Street, Melbourne VIC 3000Phone 03 9320 1082Email resolutions@BLSSA.com.au

We’ll acknowledge your complaint and work to resolve it as quickly as we can. If we can’t give you a final response within the required timeframe, we’ll explain why and tell you about your right to take the complaint to AFCA.

If you’re not satisfied with our response

You can contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), a free and independent dispute resolution scheme. We and BLSSA Pty Ltd are members.

Australian Financial Complaints AuthorityWebsite afca.org.auPhone 1800 931 678 (free call)Mail GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001

For privacy complaints, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
