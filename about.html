About P & J

Experienced. Approachable. Forward-looking.

P & J Financial Solutions was founded in 2013 by Jason Smith and Paul Channell after long careers in banking and lending. Today our team has more than 90 years of combined experience, and we still work the way we started: simply, personally and with your interests first.

Talk to our teamMeet the team

Our aim is to keep it simple.

Our job is to find the loan that best meets your needs and make the experience as easy as possible, so you understand exactly what you’re signing up for.

We offer professional, personal service to every client, whether it’s their first loan with us or their tenth. After your loan settles we’re still here to help you change or vary it, apply for a top-up, buy another property or asset, or talk through a lending or banking question.

Our mission

To provide clients with outstanding customer service and solutions to all their financial requirements.
