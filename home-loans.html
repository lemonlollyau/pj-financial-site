Home loans in Perth, arranged by experienced brokers

Whether you’re buying your first home, upgrading, investing or refinancing, we’ll find a loan that suits your situation and walk you through every step. We’ve been helping WA families buy homes since 2013, often across more than one generation.

Where are you up to?

Buying your first home

Your first purchase comes with the most questions. We’ll explain deposits, lenders mortgage insurance, any government schemes you may be eligible for and what lenders look at, before you start house-hunting.

Upgrading or downsizing

Moving home means juggling a sale and a purchase. We’ll structure the finance so the timing works, including bridging finance if you need it.

Buying an investment property

We’ll look at how a new loan fits alongside your existing debts and which lenders’ policies suit your plans for building a portfolio.

Buying land and building

Construction loans are paid out in stages as your build progresses. We’ll set up the loan and help manage the progress payments between the lender and your builder.

Refinancing

If your loan hasn’t been reviewed in a while, it may be worth comparing. We’ll weigh the costs of switching against the savings and handle the paperwork if you go ahead.

Using your equity

For renovations, consolidating debts or a deposit on an investment. We’ll show you how much you may be able to access and what the repayments would be.

What we’ll ask you for

Having these ready speeds things up. If something’s missing, don’t worry. We’ll tell you exactly what each lender needs.

Your home loan checklist

  • Photo ID, such as a driver’s licence or passport
  • Recent payslips, or two years of tax returns if you’re self-employed
  • Statements for your savings and everyday accounts
  • Details of any loans, credit cards and buy now pay later accounts
  • A rough idea of your regular living expenses
  • The contract of sale or building contract, once you have one

Common questions

How are mortgage brokers paid?

Brokers are usually paid a commission by the lender when a loan settles, and sometimes an ongoing commission while the loan runs. Before we provide credit assistance we give you our Credit Guide, which explains how we are paid. Ask us any time for the details on your loan.

How much can I borrow?

It depends on your income, expenses, existing debts and deposit, and on each lender’s own policies, which vary more than most people expect. A short conversation is usually enough for us to give you a realistic range. Our repayment calculator shows what the repayments could look like.

Do you only work with the big banks?

No. We work with the major banks and a range of specialist lenders. That gives you more options, particularly if your situation is less straightforward, such as being self-employed or recently changing jobs.

Can you help after my loan settles?

Yes. We help with loan variations, top-ups, rate reviews and your next purchase. Many of our clients have been with us for more than a decade.
