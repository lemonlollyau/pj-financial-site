Jason Smith

Finance Director

Jason co-founded P&J Financial Solutions with Paul Channell in 2013. He has more than 30 years in the finance industry, starting at R&I Bank (now Bankwest) as a trainee officer in September 1988. During his banking career he worked in back-office roles and as a senior teller, senior lending officer and business banking manager.

Jason prides himself on excellent customer service, delivering on his promises and finding the right solution for each client.

Paul Channell

Finance Director and Co-founder

Paul has more than 35 years of experience in consumer, commercial and business credit analysis, across both straightforward and complex financial products.

His strengths are analysing financial reports, preparing cash-flow forecasts and testing the assumptions behind them. He assesses industry, economic, business and personal borrower risk, and uses that to structure business and personal credit proposals that are strategic and sound.

Paul believes financial solutions should be as individual as the people who need them, and he works with a strong ethical compass to meet each client’s goals.
