Finding the right structure

Business assets can be financed in different ways. The right choice depends on how you run your business, your cash flow and your tax position, so we explain the options in plain terms and work alongside your accountant.

  • Terms matched to the working life of the asset
  • Balloon or residual payments where they suit
  • Finance for new and used assets
  • Pre-approval so you can negotiate with confidence

Common questions

Can I finance a used vehicle or machine?

Usually, yes. Lenders set limits on the age of the asset and on whether it’s bought from a dealer or a private seller, so tell us early and we’ll match you with a lender that fits.

Do you arrange personal car loans?

Yes. We arrange car loans for individuals as well as vehicle and equipment finance for businesses.

What do I need to apply?

Usually ID, details of the asset you’re buying and recent financials or bank statements. For larger amounts or newer businesses, lenders may ask for more.
