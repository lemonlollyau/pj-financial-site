What we arrange

Commercial property

Offices, shops, warehouses and factory units, whether you’re buying for your own business or as an investment. We’ll compare lenders on deposit, term and loan structure.

Business loans

Funding for equipment, expansion, fit-outs or working capital, structured around your cash flow rather than squeezed into a standard product.

Refinancing business debt

Bring several facilities together, or move to a lender that better suits where your business is today.

A proposal a lender can say yes to

A strong commercial application is more than a completed form. We analyse your financial statements, prepare cash-flow forecasts, test the assumptions behind them and structure the proposal around the risks a lender will look for.

  • Review of financial statements and tax returns
  • Cash-flow forecasting
  • Industry, business and borrower risk analysis
  • Loan structure and security options

What lenders usually ask for

  • Two years of financial statements and tax returns
  • Recent business activity statements
  • Business and personal bank statements
  • Details of existing business debts
  • For property: the contract of sale or lease details

Common questions

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with sole traders, partnerships and companies, from businesses buying their first premises to established businesses refinancing or expanding.

How long does a commercial loan take?

It depends on the lender and the complexity of the deal. Having your financials ready makes the biggest difference. Once we’ve seen the details we’ll give you a realistic timeline.

Should I involve my accountant?

Yes, and we’re happy to work with them. Your accountant can advise on tax and structure, and we’ll arrange finance to match.

Tell us what you’re planning

Have a chat with one of our brokers about your options. There’s no obligation, and you’ll get straight answers.
